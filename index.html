Introduction

Welcome to the Class Record Information Website for Eastern Samar State University. This platform is designed to make classroom management simple, accurate, and accessible by bringing all essential academic records into one organized digital hub. Through this website, users can seamlessly look up detailed student profiles, monitor daily attendance records, and track academic progress from preliminary and midterm evaluations all the way to final grades and remarks. By streamlining data management for the BSIT 2A class, this portal helps keep students and instructors informed, organized, and up to date throughout the school year.
